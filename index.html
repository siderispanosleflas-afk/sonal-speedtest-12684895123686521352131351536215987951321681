Questions

What does each number mean?

Download and upload are how much data moves per second, in megabits (Mbps). Latency is the round-trip time of a small request. Jitter is how much latency varies. Latency under load shows how much your connection slows down while it is busy, which affects calls and games.

Why is my result lower than my plan?

Wi-Fi, other devices on the network, a distant test server and a busy router all lower the result. Use a cable, close other apps, and run the test more than once.

Where is the data sent?

Test traffic goes to Cloudflare’s speed test servers. Cloudflare’s engine also reports aggregate results to Cloudflare when a test completes. Your history stays in this browser.

How much data does a test use?

A full test can use several hundred megabytes on a fast connection. The detailed results show the amount used by your last run.
